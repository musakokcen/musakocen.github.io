I play football regularly and wanted a way to record it, the way I already do for cycling and swimming. So I'm building Heatiz: start a workout on your Apple Watch, and afterwards see your GPS mapped onto the pitch you played on, with goals, assists, positions and a few movement stats layered on top.

It's still in beta on TestFlight, with the App Store release still to come.
