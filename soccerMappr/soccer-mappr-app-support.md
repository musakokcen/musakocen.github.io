# Heatiz Support

Thank you for using Heatiz! Here's everything you need to get the most out of the app.

## Getting Started

1. **Install both apps** — Heatiz includes an iPhone app and an Apple Watch app. Both are installed automatically from the App Store.
2. **Grant Health access** — On first launch, the iPhone app will ask for access to Apple Health. This is required to read your match workouts and GPS routes.
3. **Start a match from your Watch** — Open Heatiz on your Apple Watch, tap **Open Workout**, and begin your match. The watch records everything automatically.
4. **Review on iPhone** — After the match, open Heatiz on your iPhone. Your match and heatmap will appear once the data syncs from your watch.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need an Apple Watch?**  
A: Yes. The workout and GPS tracking happen on your Apple Watch. The iPhone app is for reviewing your heatmaps, match history, and stats — it does not record workouts on its own.

**Q: What Apple Watch and iPhone versions are supported?**  
A: Heatiz requires watchOS 10 or later and iOS 18 or later.

**Q: How does the heatmap work?**  
A: Your Apple Watch records continuous GPS throughout the match. After the game, Heatiz takes that GPS route and renders it as a heatmap over a soccer field, showing which zones of the pitch you covered most. You can swipe between multiple visual styles.

**Q: My match isn't showing up on iPhone. What should I do?**  
A: Make sure Health access is granted (Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Heatiz) and that iCloud is enabled. Match data syncs via CloudKit, so an internet connection is needed for the first sync. It may take a minute or two after finishing the match.

**Q: Can I rate my performance and log goals after the match?**  
A: Yes. You can log goals, assists, positions, and a personal rating — both on your Apple Watch after the match and on iPhone from the match summary screen.

**Q: Can I edit match stats on iPhone?**  
A: Yes. Open any match on iPhone and tap the edit button on the match summary screen to update your rating, goals, assists, and positions.

**Q: What does the heatmap actually show?**  
A: It shows your real GPS movement plotted on a soccer field layout. Areas you covered most appear as hot zones. You can swipe between multiple visual styles.

**Q: What movement stats does the app show?**  
A: The match summary includes distance, duration, heart rate, calories, top speed, sprints, high-speed running (HSR), and a first-half vs. second-half comparison. Movement stats that depend on the pitch boundary (sprints, HSR, speed zones) require you to draw your pitch first — tap the map card on the match screen to do this.

**Q: What is the Recovery Score?**  
A: The Recovery Score (shown on the Trends tab) is a 0–100 readiness score computed from your Apple Health data: heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, sleep, and training load. It gives you a daily snapshot of how ready your body is before a match or training session. The score personalises itself as it builds a baseline from your history.

**Q: What is the Trends tab?**  
A: The Trends tab on iPhone shows season-level charts including monthly match counts, goals and assists over time, distance per match, rating trend, and position frequency — plus your Recovery Score at the top.

**Q: Is there a home screen widget?**  
A: Yes. Heatiz includes a home screen widget that shows your most recent sessions. Long-press your home screen and add the Heatiz widget from the widget gallery.

**Q: Does the app show weather for my matches?**  
A: Yes. Heatiz fetches historical weather conditions for each match using Apple's WeatherKit framework. Weather appears automatically on the match summary screen when a GPS location is available and you have an internet connection.

**Q: What are Saved Pitches?**  
A: You can save the pitch boundary you draw for a venue so you don't have to redraw it each time you play there. Saved pitches are managed in Settings → Saved Pitches.

**Q: Can I trim a match?**  
A: Yes. From the match summary screen you can trim the match start or end time — useful if you accidentally started the workout early or forgot to stop it.

**Q: Can I share my heatmap?**  
A: Yes. On iPhone, open any match and tap the share button. You can choose to include or exclude your match stats and health data before sharing.

**Q: Where is my data stored?**  
A: Your workout data is stored in Apple Health. Match stats (goals, assists, rating, positions) sync between your devices via iCloud/CloudKit. Nothing is sent to external servers. See our [Privacy Policy](/soccer-mappr/privacy/) for details.

**Q: Does the app drain my Apple Watch battery?**  
A: Heatiz uses HealthKit's standard workout session, which is the same API used by Apple's own Workout app. Battery usage is comparable to recording a workout in the built-in Workout app. A typical 90-minute match should be fine for most Apple Watch models.

**Q: Can I use Heatiz for other sports?**  
A: Heatiz is designed specifically for soccer. The heatmap is rendered on a soccer pitch, and the stats (positions, goals, assists, sprints) are soccer-specific.

**Q: How do I delete a match?**  
A: On your Apple Watch, swipe a match in the history list and tap the trash icon. This will delete both the HealthKit workout and the match record. You can also delete the workout directly from the Apple Health or Fitness apps.

**Q: Can I change units from metric to imperial?**  
A: Yes. Go to Settings → Units to switch between metric (km, km/h) and imperial (mi, mph).
